Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about WeFix for workshop owners and vehicle owners.

What is WeFix and who is it designed for?

WeFix is a modern multi-tenant automotive SaaS platform designed for two distinct audiences: automotive repair workshop owners (merchants) who need digital job cards, invoicing, inventory, and customer management; and vehicle owners (customers) who want to discover verified local garages, track active repairs in real-time, and maintain digital vehicle maintenance records.

How does digital repair tracking work for vehicle owners?

When your vehicle is checked into a participating WeFix garage, a digital job card is created. You receive automated status updates via WhatsApp and push notifications as your vehicle moves through inspection, active repairs, quality check, and delivery readiness. You can review and approve itemized estimates directly on your phone.

Can garage owners use WeFix on mobile phones as well as computers?

Yes. WeFix is fully responsive and multi-platform. It runs seamlessly on desktop browsers, laptops, tablets, and Android smartphones. Mechanics on the workshop floor can create job cards and update repair statuses from a phone while service advisors manage billing from a front-desk computer.

Is my workshop data isolated from other garages?

Yes, completely. WeFix enforces strict multi-tenant SaaS data isolation boundaries verified at both the application and database security rules layer. No merchant can ever view another garage's jobs, invoices, customers, revenue, or mechanic records.

Is WeFix free for vehicle owners?

Yes. Vehicle owners can sign up, discover verified garages, track their repairs, approve estimates, download invoices, and use the digital warranty vault completely free.

How do WhatsApp service notifications work?

WeFix features built-in WhatsApp dispatch. When a repair status changes, an estimate is ready, an invoice is generated, or a service reminder is due, pre-composed messages with vehicle details and direct links can be sent instantly to the customer's phone in English or Malayalam.

Still Have Questions?

Our dedicated support team is available to assist garage owners with onboarding and vehicle owners with repair inquiries.
