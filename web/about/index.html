Powering Garages. Simplifying Vehicle Care.

At V-Fix, our mission is simple: to make garage management effortless for workshop owners and make vehicle servicing transparent and stress-free for car and bike owners.

The Problem We Set Out to Solve

For decades, automotive repair workshops have relied on handwritten paper job cards, physical logbooks, and scattered invoices. When customers called to check on vehicle repair status, service advisors had to physically walk into repair bays to ask mechanics what was happening. Meanwhile, vehicle owners felt anxious about repair pricing and whether work was being done properly.

Our Solution

We built V-Fix from the ground up as a cloud-native, multi-tenant SaaS platform. We combined robust workshop floor operations—digital job cards, technician allocation, inventory control, and GST invoicing—with customer transparency tools like real-time repair status tracking, digital estimate approvals, and automated service reminders.

Our Core Values

  • Absolute Data Privacy: Complete multi-tenant isolation ensures no merchant or customer data is ever exposed to unauthorized parties.
  • Zero Friction: Thoughtfully designed for real workshop conditions with high contrast, large touch targets, and offline-resilient architecture.
  • Radical Transparency: Clear estimates, verified digital history, and honest pricing build lifelong trust between workshops and vehicle owners.
