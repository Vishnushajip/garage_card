Automotive Billing & Digital Repair Estimates

Invoicing in an automotive workshop requires precision: separating replacement spare parts from labor charges, calculating taxes accurately, and offering clear digital payment records. WeFix streamlines billing from initial cost estimates to payment receipt.

Digital Estimates with Real-Time Approval

Never start repair work without customer consent. With WeFix, mechanics draft itemized estimates breaking down labor hours and parts costs. The customer receives an instant notification and can approve or request modifications with one tap.

GST-Ready Automotive Invoices

Convert approved estimates into official tax invoices automatically upon job completion. Display vehicle license plate numbers, odometer readings, garage GSTIN, and itemized HSN/SAC codes for complete compliance.

Direct WhatsApp PDF Sharing

Deliver branded, professional PDF invoices directly to customer WhatsApp numbers and emails. Customers can view, download, and archive invoices right inside their vehicle profile.
