Proactive Vehicle Maintenance & Service Reminders

Neglected vehicle maintenance leads to breakdowns, reduced fuel economy, and expensive repairs. V-Fix uses real repair data, odometer tracking, and service intervals to calculate exactly when your vehicle needs attention.

Hybrid Interval Engine: Odometer & Calendar

V-Fix evaluates both elapsed time (e.g. 6 months) and accumulated mileage (e.g. 10,000 km) to determine maintenance status: Upcoming, Due Soon, Due Now, or Overdue.

Digital Service History for Higher Resale Value

Every oil change, brake overhaul, transmission fluid swap, and tire rotation performed at a V-Fix certified garage is recorded into your immutable vehicle timeline. When it is time to sell your vehicle, showcase verified digital maintenance records to buyers.

Parts Warranty Vault

Replacement parts installed by participating garages automatically link to your digital warranty vault. Track manufacturer guarantee periods, warranty certificate numbers, and coverage expiry dates on your smartphone.

Automated WhatsApp & Push Reminders

Receive friendly alerts 14 days and 3 days before upcoming maintenance is due, with a direct link to book an appointment with your trusted local garage.
