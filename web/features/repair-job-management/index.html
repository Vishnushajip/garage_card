Digital Job Cards & Live Automotive Repair Tracking

Paper job cards get greasy, torn, and misplaced. WeFix replaces paper forms with a state-of-the-art digital repair card that connects garage managers, service technicians, and vehicle owners in real-time.

Instant Vehicle Intake & Inspection Checklist

Capture initial customer complaints, existing dent and scratch marks, fuel gauge readings, and odometer numbers right at the intake bay on a smartphone, tablet, or PC. Attach photos for complete transparency.

5-Stage Status Timeline

Jobs progress through verified operational states: Pending Inspection → In Progress → Quality Check → Ready for Delivery → Completed. Every status transition records precise timestamps and mechanic IDs for accountability.

Multi-Tenant Privacy Isolation

Internal technician observations, mechanic labor costs, and supplier parts margins remain strictly confidential inside the merchant workspace. Customers see clean, professional customer-facing updates without exposing sensitive workshop business data.

Secure Digital Gate Pass Issuance

Prevent unauthorized vehicle release. Once the repair invoice is paid and the job is marked complete, WeFix instantly generates a verifiable digital gate pass with a unique security token.
