Garage Management System Built for Peak Automotive Efficiency

Running a high-performing auto or motorcycle repair garage requires juggling vehicle check-ins, customer diagnostics, mechanic assignments, spare part requisitions, and timely delivery. V-Fix unifies these disparate operational steps into a single, intuitive cloud dashboard.

Centralized Workshop Dashboard

Gain real-time visibility into your entire garage floor. The V-Fix dashboard displays active repair volume, pending diagnostic inspections, vehicles waiting for customer estimate approval, jobs currently in bay, and vehicles scheduled for delivery today.

Customer & Fleet Record Management

Every vehicle that enters your workshop is automatically indexed with its registration number, VIN, make, model, year, and customer contact info. Technicians can view full previous repair history in one tap, preventing duplicate diagnoses and ensuring accurate repairs.

Dynamic Service Catalog with Tiered Pricing

Unlike rigid legacy tools, V-Fix allows merchants to define custom service packages (e.g. Periodic Maintenance, Brake Pad Replacement, AC Gas Top-up, Wheel Alignment) with vehicle-specific pricing tiers for hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, luxury European models, and 2-wheelers.

Multi-Technician Task Allocation

Assign specific mechanics to individual jobs. Track estimated labor completion times, inspection notes, technician signatures, and internal mechanic notes that remain private from customer-facing views.
