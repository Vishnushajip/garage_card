Privacy Policy & SaaS Multi-Tenant Data Protection

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At WeFix Technologies, data privacy and tenant boundary protection are fundamental engineering requirements. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and protect your information when using the WeFix platform.

1. Strict Multi-Tenant SaaS Isolation

WeFix operates under an absolute tenant isolation architecture. Every garage merchant's operational data—including repair job cards, billing invoices, customer rosters, mechanic labor costs, supplier pricing, and revenue reports—is cryptographically and rule-validated at the database layer. No merchant can ever query, inspect, or modify another garage's records.

2. Customer Private Data Protection

Vehicle owners maintain exclusive ownership over their vehicle registration numbers, private phone numbers, service logs, and payment histories. Discovering a garage grants access only to publicly authorized workshop catalog details (e.g. services, verified location, operating hours). It never exposes customer identities to third parties.

3. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, authenticated email, phone number, role (Customer or Merchant).
  • Workshop Information: Garage business name, verified location coordinates, public contact number, services offered, and pricing tiers.
  • Vehicle Records: License plate number, make, model, year, fuel type, and odometer readings.
  • Repair & Billing Data: Service job cards, line-item estimates, parts invoices, and payment status records.

4. Zero Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal data or garage business records to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Cloud databases enforce granular security rules requiring cryptographic authentication on all read and write transactions.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact our Privacy Officer at support@wefix.app.
