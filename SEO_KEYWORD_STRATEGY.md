## 1. Executive Summary & Search Intent Architecture

V-Fix serves two fundamentally different personas with distinct search intents:
1. **B2B / Merchant Intent (Commercial & Transactional):** Workshop owners searching for software tools to streamline job cards, mechanics, inventory, GST billing, and customer updates.
2. **B2C / Customer Intent (Local & Commercial Investigation):** Car and bike owners searching for reliable servicing, transparent repair pricing, nearby specialized mechanics, and maintenance tracking.

- **Specialized Services:** `car periodic maintenance`, `car oil change near me`, `bike brake pad replacement`, `car AC repair`, `wheel alignment and balancing`, `OBD-II computerized diagnostics`.
- **Vehicle Care & History:** `vehicle repair tracking`, `digital vehicle service logbook`, `car maintenance reminders`, `parts warranty tracker`.

### C. Local SEO Focus (Regional Hubs)
- **Primary Geographies:** Kerala, India (Calicut, Kochi, Malappuram, Thrissur, Trivandrum, Kannur).
- **Legitimate Local Intent:** Connecting vehicle owners in Kerala with authentic, registered local workshops on V-Fix without fabricating physical branch locations.
